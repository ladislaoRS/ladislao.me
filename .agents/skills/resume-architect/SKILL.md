# Resume Architect Skill

A specialized guide for creating authentic, high-impact resumes that pass ATS filters, captivate human recruiters, and look impeccable both digitally and in PDF/print format.

## 1. The Authentic Narrative Formula

Authenticity comes from specific, honest craftsmanship rather than generic buzzwords:
- **Avoid hollow buzzwords**: Replace "results-driven visionary team player" with concrete engineering challenges you solved and decisions you made.
- **The Google XYZ Impact Formula**:
  > *"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]."*
  - *Example*: "Reduced API latency by 45% (from 420ms to 230ms) across 2M daily requests by implementing redis caching and query batching in Laravel."
- **Active Power Verbs**: Architected, Engineered, Optimized, Spearheaded, Refactored, Delivered, Automated, Mentored.

## 2. Structural Blueprint

1. **Header & Contact Info**:
   - Full Name, Professional Title, Location / Remote Availability
   - Contact links: Email, LinkedIn, GitHub, Personal Website / Portfolio
2. **Executive Summary / Craft Statement**:
   - 2-3 sentences defining your engineering ethos, core strengths, and the value you bring to teams.
3. **Core Competencies / Tech Stack**:
   - Categorized cleanly (e.g., Languages, Frameworks & Libraries, Architecture & Databases, DevOps & Tools).
4. **Professional Experience**:
   - Reverse chronological order.
   - Title, Company, Location, Date Range.
   - 3-5 bullet points per role highlighting measurable impact, technical ownership, and team contributions.
5. **Featured Projects / Open Source**:
   - Project name, one-sentence description, tech stack used, key outcome or link.
6. **Education & Certifications**:
   - Degree, Institution, Graduation Year, relevant honors or certificates.

## 3. Web & Print Dual-Mode Optimization

- **Web Mode**: Interactive elements, dark/light toggle, live links, subtle hover transitions, badges.
- **Print / PDF Mode (`@media print`)**:
  - Hide non-essential navigation, interactive buttons, and footers.
  - Page-break controls: `break-inside-avoid` on job entries to prevent split cards.
  - Standard margins (0.5in / 12mm) and high-contrast typography (pure black or dark zinc on crisp white).
  - Print button (`window.print()`) that enables instant PDF generation directly from the browser.
